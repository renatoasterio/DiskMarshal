# Code of conduct

MacCleaner follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies to every space of this project: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. People here have different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
- Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully when you are wrong.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks or unwelcome personal attention, in public or in private.
- Do not share anyone's private information without permission.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/renatoasterio), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently block a participant.
